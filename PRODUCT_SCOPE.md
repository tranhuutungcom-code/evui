# Product scope and delivery gates

## User outcomes
- Around age 3: enjoy listening and voluntarily imitate sounds/words.
- Around age 5: greet and talk about familiar people/things.
- Around age 8: describe a simple daily routine in sequence.
- Around age 11: take part in age-appropriate conversations and express simple opinions with reasons.

These are instructional goals, not guaranteed outcomes based only on age or lesson completion.

## MVP scope
This starter includes 16 representative lessons (4 per level) to test the learning loop. It does NOT complete two full units per level. A complete curriculum based on the proposed V1.0 architecture is 4 levels × 8 units × 10 lessons = 320 lessons.

## Release gates
1. Pedagogy: lesson objectives, language accuracy, audio, age appropriateness reviewed.
2. Child safety: parent consent, safe content boundaries, no open-ended unsupervised AI chat.
3. Privacy: collect minimum data; avoid unnecessary full names, exact birth dates, school/location; define deletion and retention.
4. Engineering: RLS tests, auth tests, backup, monitoring, error handling, rate limiting.
5. Business: terms, privacy notice, refund/subscription rules, payment gateway and support.
